A note from Coach Ryan

How VST began.

Endless Vocals is becoming VST: year-round training with the Vocal Strength Trainer app at its heart.

VST began with Tuned XD, Jaime Vendera’s original app. Its pitch wheel, scales, two-track recorder, and glass-shattering game helped me discover my voice through his detailed exercises. But Jaime always had a bigger vision for the way singers could practice.

After years of studying his method and working alongside him on projects including Angel Fire East and Timestamped, I understood the training he wanted to bring to life. Jaime’s ear and vision are exceptional, and I’m proud to call him my mentor and friend.

VST brings that vision together: a way to practice the ISO Method, learn how your voice responds, and take the work into songs. I’m proud of what Jaime and I have built, and I can’t wait to show you how to use it.

— Coach Ryan Wall

Meet the Coaches

Meet Ryan, his mentor Jaime, and standby coach Tiago.

Head Coach & Founder

Ryan Wall

Ryan is a singer, songwriter, and vocal coach who spent a decade teaching alongside Jaime at Vendera Vocal Academy. At Endless Vocals, he helps singers work on technique and bring it into their songs. He also shares songwriting and production experience with the community.

Mentor & Originator of the ISO Method

Jaime Vendera

Jaime Vendera is an internationally recognized vocal coach, author of Raise Your Voice, creator of the ISO Method, and Ryan’s mentor. Known for his glass-shattering demonstrations and detailed approach to training, Jaime helped inspire Vocal Strength Trainer and will join the VST training as a special guest from time to time. www.jaimevendera.com

Standby Coach & Artist

Tiago Costa

Tiago is a vocalist, guitarist, and multi-instrumentalist known for his work with Firemage and Xeque Mate. He joins Endless Vocals as a standby coach, helping with classroom duties, extra clinics, and support in Discord when needed.
