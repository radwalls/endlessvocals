FIFTY-EIGHT BETTER v0.2.1 — INSTALLATION

Thank you for supporting independent music tools and vocal education.

LOGIC PRO / AUDIO UNIT
1. Open the Components folder in this download.
2. Copy Fifty-Eight Better.component to:
   Macintosh HD/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
3. Restart Logic Pro.
4. Find the plug-in under Audio FX > Fifty Eight Audio.

VST3 HOSTS
1. Open the VST3 folder in this download.
2. Copy Fifty-Eight Better.vst3 to:
   Macintosh HD/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins if needed.

Fifty-Eight Better requires macOS 11 or later and an Apple Silicon Mac.
