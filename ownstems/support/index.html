OwnStems support

Keep the music moving.

Quick answers for importing a mix, running local separation, and saving your instrumental.

For the OwnStems iPhone app

Import

A file will not open

Choose a stereo WAV, AIFF, MP3, or M4A from Files. It must be 10 minutes or shorter. If Files shows a cloud icon, download the file first.

Processing

The phone gets warm

Neural separation is demanding. Connect to power, let the iPhone cool, close memory-heavy apps, and keep OwnStems in the foreground while it works.

Result

A vocal remains

Dense reverb, doubled vocals, distortion, and sounds overlapping the vocal range can leave traces. Try Studio mode for the best available detail.

Export

I cannot find the WAV

On the result screen, use Share, then Save to Files. The export appears in the folder you choose; OwnStems does not select one for you.

Rights

Can I split a commercial song?

No. Use OwnStems only for music you made or fully control. Do not import commercial releases, streaming audio, unauthorized covers, remixes, or samples.

Purchase

How does lifetime access work?

Three successful full-song separations are included. A failure or cancellation does not count. The $9.99 lifetime unlock is a one-time App Store purchase; Restore Purchases is available.
